Shipping Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Delivery Areas

We currently deliver to all locations within Nigeria. International shipping is not available at this time.

Delivery Time

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Delivery times vary based on location:

LocationEstimated Delivery Time
Anambra State1-2 business days
Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt2-3 business days
Other States3-5 business days
Rural Areas5-7 business days

Shipping Costs

  • Free Shipping: On orders above ₦50,000
  • Standard Shipping: ₦2,000 - ₦5,000 (based on location)
  • Express Shipping: ₦5,000 - ₦10,000 (available on request)

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email or WhatsApp. You can track your order status on our website or through the courier service.

Delivery Process

  1. Order is confirmed
  2. Order is processed and packed
  3. Order is handed over to courier
  4. You receive tracking information
  5. Delivery is made to your address
  6. You receive your items

Failed Delivery

If delivery fails due to incorrect address or unavailability, we will contact you to reschedule. Additional delivery fees may apply for re-delivery.
