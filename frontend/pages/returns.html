Return & Exchange Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

30-Day Return Policy

We want you to love your purchase! If you're not completely satisfied, you can return items within 30 days of delivery.

Conditions for Returns

  • Unused and unworn
  • In the original packaging
  • With all tags attached
  • In the same condition as received

How to Return an Item

  1. Contact us via WhatsApp or email to initiate a return
  2. Provide your order number and reason for return
  3. Pack the item securely in the original packaging
  4. We will arrange for pickup or provide a return address
  5. Send the item back to us
  6. We will process your refund or exchange

Exchanges

Free size exchanges are available within 7 days of delivery. Contact us and we'll help you find the perfect size.

Refunds

  • Refunds will be processed within 7-14 business days
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method
  • Cash on Delivery orders will be refunded via bank transfer
  • Shipping fees are non-refundable (except for defective items)

Non-Returnable Items

  • Items on final sale or clearance
  • Undergarments and swimwear (for hygiene reasons)
  • Customized or altered items
  • Items damaged by misuse

Defective Items

If you receive a defective or damaged item, please contact us immediately. We will arrange for a replacement or full refund, including shipping costs.
