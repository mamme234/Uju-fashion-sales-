Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about shopping at Uju Fashion Sales

🛍️ Shopping

How do I place an order?

You can place an order by:

  • Browsing our shop and adding items to your cart
  • Proceeding to checkout and filling in your details
  • Choosing your preferred payment method (Cash on Delivery or Bank Transfer)
  • Confirming your order

You can also order directly via WhatsApp by clicking the WhatsApp button on any product.

Can I order via WhatsApp?

Yes! You can order directly via WhatsApp by clicking the WhatsApp button on our website or calling 07035585774. Just send us the product name, size, and color you want.

Do you have a physical store?

Yes! We are located at Okpuno, Awka, Anambra State, Nigeria. Feel free to visit us during business hours.

💳 Payments & Delivery

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept:

  • Cash on Delivery - Pay when you receive your items
  • Bank Transfer - Transfer to our bank account

Bank transfer details will be provided after order confirmation.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery within Nigeria typically takes 2-5 business days. You'll receive a tracking number once your order is shipped.

Delivery times may vary based on your location.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is FREE for orders above ₦50,000. For orders below ₦50,000, delivery fees may apply based on your location.

Do you deliver outside Nigeria?

Currently, we only deliver within Nigeria. International shipping is not available at this time.

🔄 Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy. Items must be unused, unworn, and in their original packaging. Please contact us before returning any items.

Can I exchange an item?

Yes! We offer free size exchanges within 7 days of delivery. Contact us to arrange an exchange.

How do I return an item?
  1. Contact us via WhatsApp or email to initiate a return
  2. Provide your order number and reason for return
  3. Pack the item securely in the original packaging
  4. We will arrange for pickup or provide a return address
  5. We will process your refund or exchange

👕 Products

What sizes do you offer?

We offer sizes from S to XXL. Please check the size guide on each product page for detailed measurements.

If you're unsure about your size, contact us and we'll help you choose the perfect fit.

Are your products authentic?

Yes! All our products are sourced from premium suppliers and are 100% authentic. We pride ourselves on delivering quality fashion that lasts.

Do you have new arrivals regularly?

Yes! We update our collection regularly with the latest trends. Follow us on Instagram and subscribe to our newsletter to stay updated.

👤 Account

How do I create an account?

Click on the user icon in the top right corner and select "Create Account". Fill in your details and you're all set!

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your email address and we'll send you a link to reset your password.

How do I view my order history?

Log in to your account and go to "My Account" → "Orders". You'll see all your past and current orders there.

Still have questions?

We're here to help! Contact us and we'll be happy to assist you.
